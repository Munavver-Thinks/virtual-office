# M1 review: Design system + base components

**Final verdict: PASS** after one fix round. Tests: 40/40 (Node 22.23.2; Node 18 and 20 are checked in CI).

## Round 1: PASS, 4 Minors

Every M1 "must be true" item was met:

- All 26 tokens per theme match the spec exactly.
- Every listed component exists with the correct semantics.
- The gallery shows both themes and all four panel states.
- The old app still works in demo mode.

| # | Finding | Outcome |

| 1 | Minor: the stale state's text wasn't dimmed. `--text` was remapped but inherited colour wasn't. | Fixed: `.ao-stale { color: var(--text-2) }` (components.css:27) |
| 2 | Minor: the hover card could flip below and cover its own focused anchor. | Fixed: `placeHover` measures all four sides, with a side fallback (components.js:182-201). New test "when neither side fits". |

Also checked and fine:

- **public/ui/package.json:** served over HTTP but harmless. It holds no secrets and is the cleanest way for the Node 18 tests to import the ESM files.
- **Escaping:** every component escapes its input. All 23 functions were fuzzed with a `<img onerror>` payload, with 0 leaks.
- **Amber and red scope:** amber appears only for needs-you and red only for errors and over budget.
- **Accessibility:** controls are 44px on touch screens, reduced motion is respected, the focus ring is 2px with a 2px offset, there are no fake buttons, and nothing scrolls sideways at 375px.

## Round 2 (re-review of the fixes): PASS, 1 new Minor

- The test case the developer removed had become wrong under the new behaviour, and the new test covers that position.

## Known trade-offs (owner to note)

- Avatar initials are dark rather than white like the reference image. White fails 4.5:1 on every project colour (1.5–3.0:1).
- In light theme, a teal fill on a bar or ring is 2.2:1 non-text contrast. That's the spec's value, and every bar and ring has visible numbers or a text alternative.
