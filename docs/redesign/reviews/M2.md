## Owner decisions applied

- **Long waits:** an agent waiting on you stays "Needs you" until the session ends or 8 hours pass, with `stale: true` after 30 minutes.
- **Plans:** `agent-office install --with-plans` sets `CLAUDE_CODE_ENABLE_TODO_TOOLS=1`. The snapshot exposes `hasPlan`.
- **Detail data:** on by default; diffs and output are dropped after 2 days.
- **Opt-out:** names, paths and counts only. Search filters and notification or error text are not sent.

## Round 2 (new Major from decision C)

Compaction of event files older than 2 days could double-count a day's cost after a crash or a locked file, and could stop the server from starting.

Fixed:
- Compaction writes a temp file and finishes with a rename.
- A day is read as the union of its files.
- Startup recovers from any partial state.
- Every failure is caught and retried at the next hourly prune.

All three original repros and 8 new cases pass.

## Open Minors (none block)

1. **Replay dedupe can merge two genuinely identical events** (store.js:507-525). The two must be identical after stripping, within the same millisecond, on a day with more than one file. Fix: count duplicates per file and keep the highest count across files.
2. **A sub-agent's question survives a restart.** A non-compact SessionStart only clears main-agent questions (store.js:175). A sub-agent waiting when Claude Code was killed stays "Needs you (stale)" for up to 8 hours.
3. **Demo before 01:30 local time shows partial "today" numbers.** The history starts yesterday evening so nothing lands in the future, and the case has no test.
